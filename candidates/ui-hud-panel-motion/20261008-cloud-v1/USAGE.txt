Feature
The dock uses a 0.28-second CUBIC ease-in-out slide when the map overview hides
the dialogue and when dialogue is restored. Reversal starts from the displayed
position. Responsive layout retains authority over the dock's resting position
and size. Old Tween callbacks are ignored. The adapter does not write draft text,
business disabled flags, world position, RNG or receipts, or call an action.
These are source-level properties; their runtime behavior remains unverified.
Only the bottom submission dock is changed; other panels are outside this patch.
